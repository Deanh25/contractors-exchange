How it works and the one thing to confirm

To match Set price exactly, the reserve is treated as the seller's net floor (what they take home), not the buyer-facing number. The panel then mirrors Set price: net, the fixed 12% margin, and the buyer figure, here labeled "Bidding opens at" since it is the minimum a buyer can bid.

The dashed "what a higher close earns you" strip is an optional extra: it makes the "potential" part concrete by showing the take at a sample winning bid. In the real form it can update live if the seller types a hypothetical bid, or we can drop it and keep only the reserve breakdown.
